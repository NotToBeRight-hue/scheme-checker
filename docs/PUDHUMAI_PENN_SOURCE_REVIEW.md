## Publication blockers and implementation decision

1. **Course-specific school history and exceptions:** class 6–12 summaries and inclusion of ITI/diploma do not establish all qualifying histories. The implementing rules are needed for shorter schooling, RTE history and progression/first-course boundaries. A single broad school-history test could reject a qualifying applicant or approve an unsupported one.
2. **Later expansion's scheme-specific allocation:** the official June 2025 release establishes a real school-history waiver but discusses Pudhumai Penn and Tamil Pudhalvan together. A Pudhumai Penn-only rule must establish the applicable route for every affected category without assigning them arbitrarily or adding the other scheme.

These are source-verification blockers. They do not demonstrate a missing operator or justify architecture/schema/security changes. The existing engine can represent the verified clauses using its supported types, AND/OR groups and existing missing-answer behavior. An unknown user answer is different from an unknown government policy: injecting an artificial unanswered question to mask a policy gap would not resolve it.

No narrow cohort, simplified rule, draft or reviewer-approved publication was substituted. The stopping decision follows Patch 4B Requirement 2: stop before publication when a mandatory criterion cannot be verified or modeled.

## Existing workflow fit and pending pilot verification

The inspected current source supports authenticated question creation, draft scheme metadata and linked questions, rule creation/validation and transactional publication in `backend/src/main/java/in/project/schemes/Api.java`. Real publication requires an official government URL, verification date, exceptions, reviewer approval and a source mapping for every condition. Patch 2 validates every linked question's active status. Patch 3 canonicalizes exact numeric rules in `RuleEngine.java`; no operator changes are needed for this pilot.

Citizen typed question inputs and answer saving are present in `frontend/src/main.tsx`; `Api.java` evaluates current answers at the expected profile revision. Existing decisive UNKNOWN returns `422 INPUT_REQUIRED` without an evaluation-history entry. Source mappings and exceptions are available through the API; essential guidance would also need to appear in existing displayed metadata fields. No frontend redesign is required.
